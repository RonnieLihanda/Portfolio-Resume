Leading delivery teams and the processes they run on

Microhealth program teams, proposal operations and AI automation.

What I do

I keep several teams working to one rhythm: clear boards, written procedures, regular reporting and early warnings when something slips.

Microhealth program teams

I have managed three delivery teams on the same program, each with its own board and written operating procedure:

  • Data team. Board design, weekly activity and hours reporting, and the reminders that keep logging complete. See the automation case study.
  • Web development team. Sprint board structure, ticket flow and a standard operating procedure for how work moves from request to release.
  • Testing team. Test tracking on its own board, with the same reporting rhythm as the other two teams.

Proposal team

I lead proposal operations: finding opportunities, breaking solicitations into compliance matrices, deciding bid or no bid, assigning sections to writers and running review before submission. More than 30 RFP responses have gone through this process. The Claude skills I built cut the time to draft a response from about a week to about two days. Details are in the RFP case study.

AI automation team

I set direction for the automation work: which processes to automate first, how workflows are built and documented, and how they are monitored once live.

How I lead

  • Write the procedure first, so new people can take over without a handover call.
  • Report on a fixed day each week, with the same headings every time.
  • Automate the chasing, so the team spends its time on the work.
  • Review output before it goes to the client, and fix the process when the same error repeats.
