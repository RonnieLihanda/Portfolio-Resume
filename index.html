Automation

Automation that does the chasing.

A data team logged its work in Jira and Tempo, and every person needed a weekly report. I replaced the chasing and the copy-pasting with three connected workflows.

  • Activity reminders. Wednesday to Friday, the people with no logged work get a nudge that firms up each day. A log stops anyone being reminded twice.
  • Weekly reports with AI drafting. Each person's report is drafted from their ticket comments, then published to Confluence and emailed as PDF.
  • Hours version. The same report with Tempo time, matched to tickets without double counting.
  • Built to fail loudly. If the email step breaks, an alert goes out instead of nothing happening.

Proposals

RFP responses in two days, not a week.

Proposal teams lose bids on compliance mistakes, not weak ideas. I built Claude skills and agents that run a response from solicitation to final check.

  • Compliance matrix. Every "shall" and "must" becomes a row, quoted word for word.
  • Go or no-go first. A short, evidence-based call with at most three win themes.
  • One section at a time. Draft, stop, approve, then move on.
  • No invented facts. Missing numbers stay as visible placeholders.
  • Review gates. Compliance, quality, coverage and a submission checklist.

More than 30 RFP responses. About a week became about two days. These are my own working-time estimates, not timesheet data.
